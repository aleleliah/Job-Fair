# Virtual Job Fair Booth

Virtual Job Fair Booth is an Android application designed to facilitate virtual job fairs. It consists of two modules:
Admin Module – For company representatives to manage their profiles, including adding, updating, and deleting company details such as job opportunities, products/services, and investor contacts.
Client Module – For job seekers to search and view company details and job listings by entering the company name.

Features
Admin Module:
Add new company profiles
Update existing company details
Delete company profiles
Firebase Realtime Database integration

Client Module
Search companies by company name

View company details such as:
-About the company
-Job opportunities
-Products and services
-Investor contacts
